MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of 1 March 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between Orion Analytics LLC, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 400 Harbor Street, Wilmington, Delaware ("Orion" or "Provider"), and Vertex Industrial Holdings Inc., a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 1120 Hudson Avenue, New York, New York ("Vertex" or "Customer").

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Change of Control" means, with respect to either party, (a) the sale of all or substantially all of such party's assets, (b) any merger, consolidation or reorganization in which such party is not the surviving entity, or (c) any transaction or series of related transactions in which any person or group acquires beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of such party.

1.2 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally, in writing or by inspection of tangible objects, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information.

1.3 "Services" means the data analytics, reporting and advisory services described in each Statement of Work executed by the parties under this Agreement.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 Initial Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of thirty-six (36) months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 Renewal. Following the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Fees. Customer shall pay Provider the fees set out in each Statement of Work. The annual minimum commitment for the Initial Term is two million four hundred thousand United States dollars ($2,400,000), payable in equal quarterly installments.

3.2 Invoicing. Provider shall invoice Customer quarterly in advance. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

3.3 Late Payment. Amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

4. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

4.1 General Prohibition. Neither party may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, whether by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other party, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

4.2 Change of Control. A Change of Control of either party shall be deemed an assignment of this Agreement requiring consent under Section 4.1. The party undergoing the Change of Control shall give the other party written notice not less than thirty (30) days before the closing of the transaction.

4.3 Consequences of an Unconsented Change of Control. If a Change of Control occurs without the prior written consent required by Section 4.2, the non-transferring party may, at its sole option and upon written notice delivered within sixty (60) days of becoming aware of the Change of Control, (a) terminate this Agreement with immediate effect, (b) suspend performance until consent is granted, or (c) require the transferee to provide a guarantee of performance reasonably satisfactory to the non-transferring party. Termination under this Section 4.3 is without liability to the terminating party and does not relieve Customer of fees accrued before the effective date of termination.

4.4 Permitted Transfers. Notwithstanding Sections 4.1 and 4.2, either party may assign this Agreement without consent to an affiliate under common control with the assigning party, provided the assigning party remains liable for all obligations under this Agreement.

5. TERMINATION

5.1 Termination for Convenience. Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon one hundred eighty (180) days' prior written notice, subject to payment of an early termination fee equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of the fees that would have become payable over the remainder of the then-current term.

5.2 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice of a material breach if the breaching party fails to cure the breach within that period.

5.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Provider shall return or destroy all Customer Confidential Information within thirty (30) days and shall deliver a final report of Services performed through the effective date of termination.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

6.1 Cap. Except as provided in Section 6.3, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid and payable by Customer under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

6.2 Exclusion of Indirect Damages. Neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, revenue or data, however caused and under any theory of liability.

6.3 Exceptions to the Cap. The limitations in Sections 6.1 and 6.2 do not apply to (a) a party's indemnification obligations under Section 8, (b) breach of confidentiality obligations under Section 7, (c) a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or (d) Customer's obligation to pay fees.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Obligation. Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of like importance, and in no event less than reasonable care.

7.2 Duration. The obligations in this Section 7 survive for five (5) years after termination of this Agreement, except with respect to trade secrets, for which the obligations survive for so long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 By Provider. Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer from any third-party claim alleging that the Services infringe any United States patent, copyright or trade secret, and shall pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

8.2 By Customer. Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider from any third-party claim arising from Customer data supplied to Provider, including any claim that such data was collected or transferred in violation of applicable law.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

9.2 Dispute Resolution. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration seated in New York, New York before a single arbitrator under the Commercial Arbitration Rules of the American Arbitration Association. Either party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to protect its Confidential Information or intellectual property.

10. NOTICES

10.1 All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses first set out above, with a copy to each party's General Counsel.

11. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

11.1 This Agreement, together with all Statements of Work, constitutes the entire agreement of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior proposals, understandings and agreements, whether oral or written.
